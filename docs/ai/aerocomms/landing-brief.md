# AeroComms Landing Brief

## File location

Place this file in the FlyPath repository at:

```text
flypath/docs/ai/aerocomms/landing-brief.md
```

This brief defines what the new AeroComms landing page should communicate, how it should be structured, and what Cursor should build.

---

# 1. Objective

Create a new landing page for AeroComms inside the FlyPath website.

The page should introduce AeroComms as FlyPath's guided ATC communications training product for future pilots and student pilots.

The landing should be premium, clean, mobile-first, and inspired by the provided reference landing, while staying clearly aligned with FlyPath's identity.

The landing should not feel like a generic SaaS page. It should feel like a trustworthy aviation training product from FlyPath.

---

# 2. Product Definition

AeroComms is a guided ATC communications trainer inside FlyPath.

It helps users practise:

- Listening
- Readbacks
- Phraseology
- Guided scenarios
- Progress tracking
- Radio confidence

AeroComms is designed for:

- Future pilots
- Student pilots
- PPL students
- ATPL/modular students
- Pilots who want to improve aviation English and radio confidence

AeroComms is not positioned as:

- An official ATC certification
- A replacement for flight instructors
- A replacement for real radio practice
- A public App Store / Google Play app, unless that changes later

---

# 3. Core Message

Primary message:

> Entrena tus comunicaciones ATC con confianza.

Supporting message:

> Practica listening, readbacks, fraseología y escenarios guiados para llegar mejor preparado a la radio real.

Alternative message:

> Tu entrenamiento de radio aeronáutica, organizado paso a paso dentro de FlyPath.

The landing should communicate that AeroComms is structured, progressive and practical.

---

# 4. Target User Pain Points

The page should connect with these problems:

- Students often know phraseology but freeze when they have to use the radio.
- Listening to ATC is harder than reading phraseology from a book.
- Readbacks require structure, speed and confidence.
- Many students do not know what to practise next.
- Radio confidence is built by repetition, context and progression.

Safe wording:

> Saber fraseología no siempre significa estar listo para la radio.

> AeroComms te ayuda a practicar antes de enfrentarte a la presión de la radio real.

## Section 9 — FAQ

Purpose: answer doubts.

Recommended questions:

### ¿Qué es AeroComms?

AeroComms es el entrenamiento de comunicaciones ATC de FlyPath. Te ayuda a practicar listening, readbacks, fraseología y escenarios guiados.

### ¿Es para pilotos con experiencia?

Está pensado sobre todo para futuros pilotos y student pilots, pero también puede ayudar a pilotos que quieran reforzar confianza y estructura en radio.

### ¿Necesito tener PPL?

No necesariamente. La idea es empezar desde una base guiada y avanzar poco a poco.

### ¿Está en español o en inglés?

La interfaz y explicaciones pueden estar en español. La fraseología y los ejemplos de radio se trabajan en inglés aeronáutico.

### ¿Es una app independiente?

AeroComms forma parte del ecosistema FlyPath. La disponibilidad exacta dependerá de la fase del producto.

### ¿Sustituye a un instructor?

No. AeroComms es una herramienta de práctica y preparación. No sustituye instrucción oficial ni entrenamiento real con instructor.

### ¿Incluye IA?

No hacer claims concretos de IA salvo que estén implementados. Si se menciona, usar una fórmula prudente como: `Estamos explorando funciones inteligentes para mejorar la práctica y el feedback.`

# 6. Copywriting Rules

Use Spanish by default.

Use English only for aviation phraseology terms or examples, such as:

- Readback
- Say again
- Cleared for takeoff
- Hold short
- Runway two four

Tone:

- Clear
- Direct
- Premium
- Realistic
- Educational
- Practical

Avoid:

- Hype
- Fake urgency
- Fake user numbers
- Fake testimonials
- Fake reviews
- Overpromising fluency or mastery
- Claims about official certification

Do not use:

- `Domina la radio en 7 días`
- `Habla como un piloto profesional desde cero`
- `La app definitiva`
- `IA revolucionaria`
- `Miles de pilotos ya lo usan`

Prefer:

- `Entrena paso a paso`
- `Gana confianza`
- `Practica antes de la radio real`
- `Mejora listening y readbacks`
- `Progresión guiada`

---

# 8. Motion Requirements

Add motion only after the static page works.

Recommended motion:

- Fade/slide on section entrance.
- Staggered card reveal.
- Floating hero mockup.
- Slight parallax in hero.
- Soft hover on cards.

Avoid:

- Heavy animations.
- Scroll hijacking.
- Complex 3D.
- Video backgrounds unless optimized.
- Anything that hurts mobile performance.
